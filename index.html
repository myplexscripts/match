Mellow Match

Match. Cascade. Repeat.

Start Over?

This erases your saved level, coins, and power-ups and begins a brand new game.

How To Play

  • Swap two adjacent tiles to line up 3 or more of the same colour.
  • Match 4 in a row for a striped rocket, an L or T shape for a wrapped tile, or 5+ in a row for a colour bomb.
  • Combine two special tiles — like two rockets, or a rocket with a colour bomb — for an even bigger blast.
  • Fill the meter before you run out of moves to clear the level and bank stars.
  • Boards can be different shapes with gaps carved in — tiles rest on top of the gaps instead of falling through.
  • Earn free power-ups after every level, or spend coins on stronger ones in the Shop.
