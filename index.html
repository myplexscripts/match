How To Play

The goal is simple. Make matches, score points, and fill the star meter before you run out of moves.

The basics

Match 3 or more Swap two neighbouring tiles to line up 3 or more of the same colour. Bigger matches make stronger special tiles.
Fill the meter Every match adds score. Reach the level goal before your moves run out to clear the level and bank your stars.
Use power-ups when you need a hand Power-ups sit below the board. Tap one to use it. You earn free ones as you play, and you can buy more in the shop.

Some boards have gaps. Tiles stop on blocked spaces, so the board will not always fill like a perfect rectangle.

Special tiles you can make on the board

Horizontal ClearMake 4 in a vertical line to create this tile. When that tile becomes part of a match, it clears the whole row.
Vertical ClearMake 4 in a horizontal line to create this tile. When that tile becomes part of a match, it clears the whole column.
Burst TileMake an L or T shape. When the burst tile becomes part of a match, it clears the tiles around it in a 3 by 3 area.
Colour BombMake 5 in a row to create it. Tap it to clear a random colour, or swap it with another tile for a bigger effect.

Special combos

Combine specials for bigger clears Two special tiles together are stronger than one on its own. Try a horizontal clear with a vertical clear, two burst tiles, or a colour bomb with another special tile.
